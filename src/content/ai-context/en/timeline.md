# Timeline_bio.md — Seina Timeline

---

## 1969 — Born in Kokura, Kitakyushu
Born in Kokura, Kitakyushu, Fukuoka Prefecture. Her father moved from piano teacher to natural food store owner. She spent her early childhood in Moji-Ōsato, near Gosho Shrine, which has deep ties to the Heike clan.

## 1981 — Enters Baiko Jo Gakuin Junior High
The school she escaped into, fleeing the bullying of elementary school. Founded by Henry Stout, who succeeded Guido Verbeck.

## 1987 — Joins Teisan Kanko, quits soon after, returns to Kitakyushu
After graduating high school, she took a job with a bus company in Kyoto. She quit soon after and returned to Kitakyushu, working at the snack bar "Meimu (Mame)" while going out to discos and soul bars.

## 1989 — Goes to New York. Meets Lucky, begins singing lessons
Goes to NY at twenty. After working at a restaurant on Long Island, moves to Manhattan. Her lessons begin with "Over the Rainbow."

## c. 1991 — Discovers Desilu
While living in the East Village, she discovers *I Love Lucy* and becomes a huge fan. She wanted to be Lucy.

## 1996 — Red Blazer, Too / Meets Leonard Gaskin
Walks into the club alone, suddenly sings, and joins Sol Yaged's band. Meets Leonard Gaskin, bassist in Sam Ulano's band.

## 1996–1999 — *I Have a Dream… Seina* — Recording and sending to nursing homes
Records World War II–era songs with Leonard Gaskin, Grady Tate, and Artie Baker. In December 1999, ships the CD to more than 3,500 nursing homes across the US. Grady Tate was the drummer she idolized above all others. In fall 1999, launches a website for this project.
→ https://japoneson.com/angelsswing/dream

## 1999 — 100 Gold Fingers Tour (1st time)
Follows Grady's tour. First meets Bob Cranshaw on this tour.

## 1999–2000 — New Year in Cape Cod
Spends the New Year in Cape Cod, then returns to Japan soon after.

## 2000 — Featured in Ryu Murakami's email newsletter
→ https://japoneson.com/angelsswing/dream/ryu

## 2000 — Travels through India and Italy
India (Sai Baba's ashram) → too much culture shock in India, gives up partway and goes to Milan → travels south through Italy for a month. Meets Kunio, her future husband, in India.

## 2001 — 100 Gold Fingers Tour (2nd time). Moves to Tokyo/Ikebukuro
The biennial tour's second run. Reunites with Grady and Bob. That same year, lives in Tokyo, working in Ginza and Ikebukuro.

## c. 2002 — Second trip to India
Thailand → Malaysia → Singapore → Sumatra → gets an Indian visa in Bangkok → India.

## c. 2003 — Marries. Moves to Suwahara Danchi, Wako City, Saitama
Near the former site of Camp Drake, a US Army base once used by returning Korean War veterans. RIKEN (the Institute of Physical and Chemical Research) is close by.

## c. 2006 — Divorces. Three months in New York
Goes to NY right after the divorce. Learns Leonard is in a nursing home. Meets Wade Barns.

## 2006–2007 — Contract work at NetApp (General Affairs)
General affairs work at an IT company.

## 2008 — Records "I'll Be Seeing You" with Leonard Gaskin / Nursing home concert
Leaves NetApp and performs a concert at Leonard's nursing home, backed by Bob Cranshaw and Local 802.
→ https://japoneson.com/angelsswing/leonard-gaskin

## 2008 — Yokohama show
A small venue.

## 2008–2011 — Reunites with Bob every time he tours Japan
One year, Bob offers: "If you're making a demo, come to New York — I'll back you up." But Seina, worried that entering the music business would make her unable to sing again, answers, "I want to record this for the memories," and they agree to record the following year. She calls Grady, who happily agrees.

## 2008 — Opens CRYSTALLIZE
Opens with a spiritual focus at its core.

## 2009 — CRYSTALLIZE / Begins exploring aromatherapy and emotional release
Starts aromatherapy after discovering Young Living essential oils. "Emotional release through aroma." Begins building a website on WordPress.

## 2010.7 — Seminar on Ken Honda's *Teachings of a Jewish Billionaire*
Made it down to 28 out of more than 5,000 applicants, but felt a strong sense of unease.

## 2011.5 — Moves to Kokura after the Great East Japan Earthquake

## c. 2011 — Reconnects with Donald Versaw online
He had come to Japan through a program inviting former POWs. A photo he sends shows Kyoto's Ryozen Kannon, and she learns that a registry of former POWs is kept there.
→ https://japoneson.com/mystery/macarthur/donald-versaw

## 2011.12 — Records "Thanks for the Memory" in NY
Piano: John Colianni. Bass: Bob Cranshaw. Drums: Leroy Williams. Grady sits this one out, on Bob's judgment.
→ https://japoneson.com/en/angelsswing/memory

## 2011–2012 — Following the trail of the Pacific War
Kyoto's Ryozen Kannon, Nagasaki (Clifton Truman Daniel, grandson of President Truman), the World Peace Pagoda, remains recovery in Okinawa, the program inviting former POWs.

## 2012.7 — US Consulate General Fukuoka Independence Day ceremony / Sings both national anthems
Sings "Kimigayo" in Japanese and "The Star-Spangled Banner" in English.

## 2012.12 — Produces a booklet on wartime and postwar Kitakyushu
Researches the Korean War memorial. Meets Monica Lewis, which leads to a connection with David Valley.

## 2013.2 — Completes the wartime/postwar Kitakyushu booklet
Distributes it at an event marking Kitakyushu's 50th anniversary as a city.

## 2013.5 — Trip through the US / Hawaii, NY, Norfolk, California, DC

## 2013.7 — US Consulate General Fukuoka Independence Day ceremony / Sings solo for the second year running
